The Challenge

Mind Creative needed a dashboard that could surface the right metrics at the right time. Their clients were drowning in data but starving for insight — raw numbers without context, charts without clarity.

The goal was to build a SaaS product that turns analytics into action — with AI layered on top to surface what matters before anyone has to ask.

What We Built
  • Real-time analytics dashboard with customizable widgets
  • AI-powered insight engine with natural language summaries
  • Multi-tenant architecture for client management
  • Data visualization library with interactive charts
  • Alert system with threshold-based notifications
  • Export and reporting with scheduled delivery
