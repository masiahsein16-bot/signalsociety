The Challenge

Sorelle Skincare needed more than a template store. They needed a digital experience that matched the premium quality of their products — one that guides customers from curiosity to checkout without friction.

The existing setup lacked brand consistency, mobile optimization, and the backend infrastructure to scale with growing demand.

What We Built
  • Brand-aligned UX/UI design system
  • Responsive e-commerce frontend
  • Product catalog with filtering and search
  • Secure payment gateway integration
  • Inventory management dashboard
  • Order tracking and customer portal
