A custom point-of-sale system built for a multi-outlet coffee shop. Real-time order management, inventory tracking, and sales analytics — all in one dashboard.

ServiceCoffee Shop POS
ScopePOS · Dashboard · Real-time Sync
IndustryFood & Beverage
The Challenge

Moora Coffee was running on off-the-shelf POS hardware with limited software flexibility. As they expanded to multiple outlets, they needed a system that could handle real-time sync across locations, track inventory centrally, and give management clear visibility into sales performance.

The existing setup couldn't scale — orders were slow, data was siloed, and reporting was manual.

What We Built
  • Multi-outlet POS interface with real-time sync
  • Menu management with modifiers and combos
  • Inventory tracking across all locations
  • Sales dashboard with daily/weekly/monthly views
  • Staff management and shift reporting
  • Receipt printing and kitchen display integration
