The Challenge

Noora Nexus was managing inventory, orders, and finances across multiple spreadsheets and disconnected tools. Data was duplicated, errors were frequent, and the team spent more time reconciling numbers than making decisions.

They needed a single source of truth — an ERP that centralizes operations without the complexity of enterprise-grade software.

What We Built
  • Unified ERP dashboard with role-based access
  • Inventory management with real-time stock levels
  • Procurement workflow with approval chains
  • Finance module with invoice and payment tracking
  • Data migration from existing spreadsheets
  • Automated reporting and alert system
